About Me

I am deeply interested in game design and the philosophy of games & sport, which goes hand in hand with my passion for sociology & politics. This enables me to create seamless worlds that are vividly life-like.

I am a political science graduate and I've been running TTRPGs since 2018, starting out with Cyberpunk 2020, followed by Cyberpunk RED.

I've tried my hand at CY_BORG & Mork Borg. I've played and ran ECOPUNK 2044, Nights Black Agents, FIST and other indie titles.

Right now, I'm deeply invested in Vampire: The Requiem after a 1 year stint into Vampire: The Masquerade.

My obsession is creating and refining mechanics that are specifically useful for specific players.

Philosophy

I believe that there is no perfect system, only good systems for specific players. This is also my belief politically, where I align myself with the anarchist movement, doing my part in that.
